Alipay vulnerability window (2009–present)
CONCLUSION

Alipay's APK signing certificate was issued on 2009-12-16 using md5WithRSAEncryption with RSA-1024 — algorithms that were already broken or deprecated at the time of issuance. The certificate remains in active use 17 years later, spanning the entire era of practical MD5 collisions, SHA-1 collisions, and RSA-1024 factorization threats.

Alipay's certificate was issued AFTER MD5 was broken, and remains in use 17 years later.
